# Follow-up tactic library

Each tactic is used at most once per deal. "Needs" lists the evidence you must verify during this run before using it. "Adds" is the new value the client gets; if you cannot deliver it, the tactic does not apply.

## Momentum

| Tactic | Fits when | Needs | Adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `product_suggestion` | The client seems unsure on product, or ignored a product already shared | Up to three products confirmed in the catalog and priced in the quoter this run, within the stated budget or near the last quoted price | A better fit on price, look or quality |
| `curated_design_idea` | The design is early or the theme is unclear | A real gallery design or filter link opened this run | One concrete creative direction |
| `offer_art_team` | Many proofs or revisions for the same event | Proof and revision history on the deal | Free art-team help and free revisions; never claim work has started |
| `expertise_value_add` | The client is weighing a decision such as fabric, print method or fit, e.g. two proofs of one design with different print types | Facts observed on the proofs or catalog | Grounded insight that makes the decision easier |

## Relevance

| Tactic | Fits when | Needs | Adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `their_deadline` | The client gave a need-by date and verified delivery requires ordering within about four weeks | Delivery dates from the proof or quoter this run | The real place-by date for their own deadline |
| `stock_warning` | A product on a recent proof is low on specific sizes or colors | Live stock checker result this run | A soft heads-up before sizes run out |
| `relevance_timing` | No hard deadline, but a real season or event from the conversation makes timing relevant | The event or season in the conversation or deal | Why now is a good time, without inventing a deadline |
| `social_proof` | The client's organization has a real chapter or peer gallery page | That page opened this run | What similar groups made, as a link; never invent stories |

## Honesty

| Tactic | Fits when | Needs | Adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `check_on_product` | The client may doubt fit, feel or quality | A real product observed this run; a free blank sample only when [the samples guide](../../account-manager/references/samples.md) allows it | A direct, easy way to resolve product doubt |
| `check_on_price` | The client seems price sensitive | A cheaper product or print option priced this run, or this deal's current promo freshly verified under [Promo Codes](../../promo-codes/SKILL.md), including database expiry and usage | A lower verified price or a newly useful existing deal offer; never create or replace a code during follow-up, or repeat an unchanged offer |
| `decision_simplification` | Many proofs or options look overwhelming | The proofs and options already shared | One recommended path, plus a new design link when one helps |
| `offer_alternative` | The current path is stuck on budget, minimum, stock, design or timing | A verified replacement product, design or timeline | A way around the blocker; prefer this over closing when one exists |

## Decision

| Tactic | Fits when | Needs | Adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `warn_expedited_delivery` | The need-by date is close and paid faster shipping can still make it | Expedited option, place-by date and cost from the quoter or proof this run | The last real way to hit their date |
| `offer_flash_shipping` | Even expedited is too late, but a Flash-eligible product can make the date | Verified Flash eligibility and delivery date this run; confirm they still need that date | A fast alternative that still works |
| `close_the_loop` | Long silence and no grounded way left to unblock the order | The conversation and history | One short, easy question on whether they are still planning the order |
| `offer_someone_else` | The client already ignored `close_the_loop` | `close_the_loop` in the follow-up history | An easy way to loop in whoever is deciding |

## Re-engage

| Tactic | Fits when | Needs | Adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `check_in_after_event` | The conversation or need-by shows the event is over | The event date | A brief check-in and an open door for the next merch; never claim the event went well |
| `future_planning` | This order did not happen | The conversation | Help planning the next round or semester |
| `account_order_history` | A similar order was placed around this time in a past cycle | That prior order visible in the deal's activity or order history | A timely reminder based on their own history |
| `conversation_mentioned_order` | The client mentioned a second order that never progressed | That order named in the conversation | A fresh start on the order they still might want |

In Re-engage, do not re-quote a stale price or deadline as current, and use no stock warnings, expedited shipping or close-out language. If a verified deadline is still achievable, say so honestly instead.
