# Follow-up tactic library

Each tactic is used at most once per deal. **Needs** lists the evidence you must verify during this run before using it. **Adds** is the new value the client gets; if you cannot deliver it, the tactic does not apply. **How** is the craft that makes it land, and **Example** shows the shape and voice only: never copy it, and replace every bracket with verified facts. Each tactic also lists the moves that break it.

## Momentum

### `product_suggestion`
- **Fits when:** the client seems unsure on product, or ignored a product already shared.
- **Needs:** up to three products confirmed in the catalog and priced in the quoter this run, within the stated budget or near the last quoted price.
- **Adds:** a better fit on price, look or quality.
- **How:** lead with your pick and say in one clause why it is your pick for them. Give every option its product link and a price that names the quantity and print type. If an earlier pick went unanswered, lead with a different one.
- **Example:** "For your rush tees I'd go with the [product], since it's softer and still [price] each for 50 with screen print."
- **Don't:** list options without a recommendation, or quote a price without its print type and quantity.

### `curated_design_idea`
- **Fits when:** the design is early or the theme is unclear.
- **Needs:** a real gallery design or filter link opened this run.
- **Adds:** one concrete creative direction.
- **How:** tie the idea to their event or organization, give at least one exact gallery link, and say in a few words what makes it work for them.
- **Example:** "Found a [theme] design that would look great for [event]. The bold back print reads well from far away: [link]"
- **Don't:** offer edits to their existing artwork, or describe a design you did not open.

### `offer_art_team`
- **Fits when:** there have been many proofs or revisions for the same event.
- **Needs:** proof and revision history on the deal.
- **Adds:** free art-team help and free revisions.
- **How:** one line of empathy, then the offer: our designers can take it from here for free, with free revisions, usually within about a day.
- **Example:** "Getting a design just right can take a few rounds. If it helps, our designers can take a pass at it for free, with as many revisions as you need."
- **Don't:** claim work has started or name a delivery time you have not verified.

### `expertise_value_add`
- **Fits when:** the client is weighing a decision such as fabric, print method or fit, for example two proofs of one design with different print types.
- **Needs:** facts observed on the proofs or catalog.
- **Adds:** grounded insight that makes the decision easier.
- **How:** name the decision, give the one fact that settles it for their use, and recommend.
- **Example:** "Between your two proofs, embroidery holds up better wash after wash, so for a gift you'd keep for years I'd go that way."
- **Don't:** lecture, or compare more than the two paths they are weighing.

## Relevance

### `their_deadline`
- **Fits when:** the client gave a need-by date and verified delivery requires ordering within about four weeks.
- **Needs:** delivery dates from the proof or quoter this run.
- **Adds:** the real place-by date for their own deadline.
- **How:** open gently, give the place-by date, and say briefly why (production plus shipping time). Frame dates as estimates.
- **Example:** "I don't want to rush you, but to have these by [need-by date] you'd want to order by about [place-by date], since printing and shipping take around [time]."
- **Don't:** use it on a date the client never confirmed, or present an estimate as a guarantee.

### `stock_warning`
- **Fits when:** a product on a recent proof is low on specific sizes or colors.
- **Needs:** a live stock checker result this run.
- **Adds:** a soft heads-up before sizes run out.
- **How:** name the product, the color and the sizes that are low, and keep it a heads-up, not a threat.
- **Example:** "Quick heads-up: the [color] [product] on your proof is running low in [sizes]."
- **Don't:** give inventory counts, or warn about stock you did not check this run.

### `relevance_timing`
- **Fits when:** there is no hard deadline, but a real season or event from the conversation makes timing relevant.
- **Needs:** the event or season in the conversation or deal.
- **Adds:** why now is a good time, without inventing a deadline.
- **How:** connect the order to the client's own event or season and to what starting now makes easy.
- **Example:** "With [event] coming up after break, getting the design locked in now leaves plenty of room for sizes and changes."
- **Don't:** use generic lines such as "merch season is here", or imply a deadline nobody set.

### `social_proof`
- **Fits when:** the client's organization has a real chapter or peer gallery page.
- **Needs:** that page opened this run.
- **Adds:** what similar groups made, as a link.
- **How:** one sentence and the link, framed as inspiration.
- **Example:** "Other [organization] chapters have done some fun ones, if you want ideas: [link]"
- **Don't:** invent stories, numbers or orders, or name another client.

## Honesty

### `check_on_product`
- **Fits when:** the client may doubt fit, feel or quality.
- **Needs:** a real product observed this run; a free blank sample only when [the samples guide](../../samples/SKILL.md) allows it.
- **Adds:** a direct, easy way to resolve product doubt.
- **How:** name the product and offer the concrete way to resolve the doubt: a fit or fabric fact, or a free blank sample of that exact item.
- **Example:** "If you're not sure how the [product] fits, I can send you a free blank one to try first."
- **Don't:** offer a sample without naming the product, or offer one the samples guide does not allow.

### `check_on_price`
- **Fits when:** the client seems price sensitive.
- **Needs:** a cheaper product or print option priced this run, or this deal's current promo freshly verified under [Promo Codes](../../promo-codes/SKILL.md), including database expiry and usage.
- **Adds:** a lower verified price or a newly useful existing deal offer.
- **How:** name the cheaper option with its price, quantity and print type, then ask one open question about fit.
- **Example:** "If budget's the thing, the [product] comes in at [price] each for 40 with one-color print, about [savings] less per shirt."
- **Don't:** ask about budget without an option to show, create or replace a code during follow-up, or repeat an unchanged offer.

### `decision_simplification`
- **Fits when:** many proofs or options look overwhelming.
- **Needs:** the proofs and options already shared.
- **Adds:** one recommended path, plus a new design link when one helps.
- **How:** pick one path, say why in a clause, and make the next step obvious.
- **Example:** "Out of everything we've looked at, I'd go with [option] for [reason]. Want me to get that one ready to order?"
- **Don't:** recap every option again.

### `offer_alternative`
- **Fits when:** the current path is stuck on budget, minimum, stock, design or timing.
- **Needs:** a verified replacement product, design or timeline.
- **Adds:** a way around the blocker. Prefer this over closing when one exists.
- **How:** name what blocks the current path in one clause, then offer one grounded swap.
- **Example:** "The [product] can't make [date], but the [alternative] can, at [price] each."
- **Don't:** offer a vague "other options" without a priced, verified one.

## Decision

### `warn_expedited_delivery`
- **Fits when:** the need-by date is close and paid faster shipping can still make it.
- **Needs:** the expedited option, place-by date and cost from the quoter or proof this run.
- **Adds:** the last real way to hit their date.
- **How:** offer only the shipping speed that makes their date, with its place-by date and extra cost.
- **Example:** "To still have these for [date], faster shipping would get them there if you order by [place-by date]. It adds [cost]."
- **Don't:** list every shipping tier, or promise a date that is not achievable.

### `offer_flash_shipping`
- **Fits when:** even expedited is too late, but a Flash-eligible product can make the date.
- **Needs:** verified Flash eligibility and delivery date this run; confirm they still need that date.
- **Adds:** a fast alternative that still works.
- **How:** recommend one Flash product that makes the date and ask whether the date still stands.
- **Example:** "Your current pick can't make [date] anymore, but the [Flash product] can. Is [date] still the day you need them?"
- **Don't:** offer several Flash products.

### `close_the_loop`
- **Fits when:** there has been long silence and no grounded way is left to unblock the order.
- **Needs:** the conversation and history.
- **Adds:** one short, easy question on whether they are still planning the order.
- **How:** two or three sentences. Include one concrete fact about their order, then the easy question. A situation either/or is fine here.
- **Example:** "Your [product] design is still saved and ready whenever you are. Should I keep it open, or close it out for now?"
- **Don't:** use guilt, a deadline you made up, or a long pitch.

### `offer_someone_else`
- **Fits when:** the client already ignored `close_the_loop`.
- **Needs:** `close_the_loop` in the follow-up history.
- **Adds:** an easy way to loop in whoever is deciding.
- **How:** one line offering to send the details to whoever is deciding or paying.
- **Example:** "If someone else is making the call on this, happy to send them the details directly."
- **Don't:** suggest the client is the problem.

## Re-engage

### `check_in_after_event`
- **Fits when:** the conversation or need-by date shows the event is over.
- **Needs:** the event date.
- **Adds:** a brief check-in and an open door for the next merch.
- **How:** name their event, and keep the door open for the next one.
- **Example:** "Hope [event] was a blast! If you've got anything coming up next semester, I'd love to help."
- **Don't:** claim the event went well.

### `future_planning`
- **Fits when:** this order did not happen.
- **Needs:** the conversation.
- **Adds:** help planning the next round or semester.
- **How:** point at the next natural occasion for their group and offer one easy first step.
- **Example:** "Is [group] planning anything for [next season]? I can pull a few ideas together whenever you're ready."
- **Don't:** re-pitch the old order or its old price.

### `account_order_history`
- **Fits when:** a similar order was placed around this time in a past cycle.
- **Needs:** that prior order visible in the deal's activity or order history.
- **Adds:** a timely reminder based on their own history.
- **How:** mention what they ordered last time and when, and offer to start this year's version.
- **Example:** "Last year you got [product] for [event] around now. Want me to start on this year's?"
- **Don't:** quote last year's price as current.

### `conversation_mentioned_order`
- **Fits when:** the client mentioned a second order that never progressed.
- **Needs:** that order named in the conversation.
- **Adds:** a fresh start on the order they still might want.
- **How:** name the order they mentioned and offer one easy first step.
- **Example:** "You'd mentioned wanting [item] for [purpose] too. Want me to put a couple of options together?"
- **Don't:** pretend it was agreed.

In Re-engage, do not re-quote a stale price or deadline as current, and use no stock warnings, expedited shipping, close-out language or "reply YES" asks. If a verified deadline is still achievable, say so honestly instead.
